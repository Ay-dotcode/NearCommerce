# Privacy Policy for Near Commerce

**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

Near Commerce ("we", "our", or "us") provides a local commerce platform connecting shoppers with nearby physical stores. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web portal and mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you sign up using email or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and account role (Customer or Store Owner).
- **Store & Product Data (Store Owners):** Store names, addresses, operating hours, product listings, pricing, and stock status.
- **Location Data:** With your permission, we process your device's geographical coordinates (GPS) or postal code to calculate proximity to nearby stores. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Photos (Shoppers):** Images captured or uploaded via the visual search feature to identify products using AI.
- **Reviews & Shared Lists:** Product and store reviews, ratings, and items in shared shopping lists.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide core platform functionality (store discovery, product search, shared household lists).
- To authenticate accounts securely via password or Google Sign-In.
- To calculate distances to nearby physical stores.
- To display public store inventory and customer reviews.

---

### 3. Google OAuth & Third-Party Services

- **Google Sign-In:** When you use "Continue with Google", we access only basic profile information (`openid`, `email`, `profile`). We do not access your Google contacts, Google Drive, or any other personal Google data.
- **No Selling of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising brokers.

---

### 4. Data Security & Retention

We implement standard security measures including encrypted JWT sessions, password hashing (bcrypt), and rate limiting to protect your account. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.
