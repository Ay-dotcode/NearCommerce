Near Commerce Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Near Commerce ("we", "our", or "us") provides a modular local commerce platform connecting shoppers with nearby physical stores. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information When you register or sign in via email or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and role (Shopper or Store Owner).
  • Store & Catalog Data (Store Owners) Store names, physical addresses, operating schedules, catalog listings, pricing, and stock verification timestamps.
  • Geolocation Data (Shoppers) With your explicit permission, we use your device coordinates (GPS) or postal code solely to display nearby stores and calculate distance. We never track your background location.
  • Camera & Visual Search Data Images submitted to our visual search feature are analyzed in real time with AI to recognize products and find nearby stock.
  • Reviews & Collaborative Lists Customer ratings, verified reviews, and items added to shared household lists.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Power Core Discovery: Calculate proximity to local physical stores and display inventory availability.
  • Secure User Authentication: Verify accounts, safeguard sessions, and support passwordless Google Sign-In.
  • Real-Time Synchronization: Sync collaborative household shopping lists across devices.

3. Google Sign-In & Third Parties

When you choose to sign in with Google, we request access only to your standard profile information (openid, email, and profile).

We never access your Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or any non-essential Google account data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security & Your Rights

All authentication requests use encrypted tokens (JWT), industry-standard password hashing (bcrypt), and automated rate-limiting to prevent unauthorized access.

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us or open an issue on our GitHub repository:
