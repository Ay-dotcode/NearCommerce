Privacy Policy for Near Commerce

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Near Commerce ("we", "our", or "us") provides a local commerce platform connecting shoppers with nearby physical stores. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web portal and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via email or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and role (Shopper or Store Owner).
  • Store & Product Data: Store names, addresses, operating hours, catalog listings, pricing, and stock status.
  • Location Data: Device coordinates (GPS) or postal code used only to locate nearby physical stores. We do not track location in the background.
  • Photos: Product photos submitted to the AI visual search feature.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to facilitate local store and product discovery, manage shared shopping lists, verify merchant listings, and authenticate user accounts.

3. Google Sign-In Data

We access only standard profile information (openid, email, profile). We do not request, access, or store contacts, drives, or other private Google services, nor do we sell or share user data with advertisers.

4. Contact Us

For questions or data deletion requests, visit our project repository at https://github.com/Ay-dotcode/NearCommerce.
